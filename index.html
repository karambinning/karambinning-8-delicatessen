A note on the place

Eight, on Gordon Road.

Gordon Road is one of those quieter West Bridgford streets where the shop is a small thing — a door, a window, a counter inside — and №8 is one of them. The kind of corner you walk past for a week, then walk into, and then keep coming back to.

The shop trades on what you'd hope a neighbourhood delicatessen does: provisions chosen one at a time, the kind of small selection that means someone made a decision. There is no menu printed on this page because the right one is the one written up inside, the morning you visit.
